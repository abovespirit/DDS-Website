SENSORY STEPS.   New class  Friday nights 

Dance without limits is A sensory-friendly dance class. This class is designed for individuals who thrive in an environment that focuses on multi-sensory instruction. This includes but is not limited to, visual support, kinesthetic support, and auditory support.

This class has an emphasis on confidence-building, direction following, and leadership development.
These strategies accommodate the students who may or may not show signs of Autism Spectrum Disorder, Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder, and other neurodivergence’s.
